📊 Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard | SQL + Power BI

I recently completed an Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard using Power BI.

In this project, I analyzed insurance data and used SQL to validate the results shown in the dashboard.

**SQL Queries I Practiced:**

1. Total Policies ?

SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT ID) AS Total_Policies
FROM insurance_data_car;

2. Total Claim Amount ?

SELECT SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim_Amount
FROM insurance_data_car;

3. Average Claim Frequency ?

SELECT AVG(`Claim Freq`) AS Avg_Claim_Frequency
FROM insurance_data_car;

4. Average Claim Amount ?

SELECT AVG(`Claim Amount`) AS Avg_Claim_Amount
FROM insurance_data_car;

5. Claim Amount by Car Use ?

SELECT `Car Use`, SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY `Car Use`;

6. Claim Amount by Car Make ?

SELECT `Car Make`, SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY `Car Make`;

7. Claim Amount by Coverage Zone ?

SELECT `Coverage Zone`, SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY `Coverage Zone`;

8. Claim Amount by Age Group ?

SELECT `Age group`, SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY `Age group`;

9. Claim Amount by Car Year ?

SELECT `Car Year`, SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY `Car Year`;

10. Claim Amount by Kids Driving ?

SELECT `Kids D`, SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY `Kids D`;

11. Claim Amount by Education ?

SELECT Education, SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY Education;

12. Claim Amount by Education and Marital Status ?

SELECT Education, `Marital Status`,
       SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY Education, `Marital Status`;

13. Total Records and Unique Policies ?

SELECT COUNT(*) AS Total_Records,
       COUNT(DISTINCT ID) AS Unique_Policies
FROM insurance_data_car;

14. Check Missing Claim Amounts ?

SELECT COUNT(*) AS Missing_Claims
FROM insurance_data_car
WHERE `Claim Amount` IS NULL;

15. Check Duplicate Policy IDs ?

SELECT ID, COUNT(*) AS Duplicate_Count
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY ID
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;

16. Claim Analysis by Car Use ?

SELECT `Car Use`,
       COUNT(*) AS Total_Records,
       SUM(`Claim Amount`) AS Total_Claim,
       AVG(`Claim Amount`) AS Avg_Claim
FROM insurance_data_car
GROUP BY `Car Use`;
