## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing insurance data using SQL and Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard provides insights into insurance policies, claim amounts, claim frequency, and different factors related to insurance claims.

I used SQL queries to analyze the data and validate the results displayed in the Power BI dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total insurance policies and claim amounts.
- Understand claim patterns across different categories.
- Compare claim amounts by car usage, car make, and coverage zone.
- Analyze claims across age groups and genders.
- Explore claim amounts by car manufacturing year and kids driving.
- Compare claims based on education and marital status.
- Validate dashboard figures using SQL queries.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization.
- **SQL (MySQL)** – Data analysis, aggregation, grouping, and data validation.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **KPI Cards:** Total policies, total claim amount, and average claim frequency.
- **Car Usage Analysis:** Claim amount by car use.
- **Car Make Analysis:** Comparison of claims across car manufacturers.
- **Coverage Zone Analysis:** Claim distribution by coverage zone.
- **Demographic Analysis:** Claim amounts by age group and gender.
- **Car Year Analysis:** Claim trends by car manufacturing year.
- **Kids Driving Analysis:** Claim amounts by kids driving category.
- **Education & Marital Status Analysis:** Comparison of claims across demographic segments.

## 🧮 SQL Analysis

SQL queries were used to analyze and validate dashboard data, including:

- Counting total policies and records.
- Calculating total and average claim amounts.
- Calculating average claim frequency.
- Grouping claim amounts by different categories.
- Checking missing claim amounts.
- Identifying duplicate policy IDs.
